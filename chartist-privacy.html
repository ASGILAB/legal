Privacy Policy — ChartPro

Last updated: 19 September 2026

ChartPro is made by ASGILAB. This page explains what we collect, why, who handles it, and how to have it removed.

The short version. There are no passwords and no profile. To purchase, you give us an email address; that is how we find your purchase again. Your card details never reach us. We do not sell your data, and we do not show ads.

What we collect, and why

  • Your email address. Entered when you sign in or purchase. We keep it in your browser and on our server, next to your plan and your scan-credit balance, so the app can tell what you have paid for.
  • Sign-in codes and sessions. To prove an email address is yours, we email it a six-digit code. The code is stored only as a hash and is deleted when used or after 10 minutes. Signing in creates a session that stays on this device for up to 90 days; only a hash of it is kept on our server, and signing out deletes it.
  • Payment details. We never see or store these. Checkout happens on a page hosted by Dodo Payments, who take the payment and send us a confirmation containing your email and what you purchased.
  • Tickers and searches. When you type a ticker or a company name, that text is sent to our server so it can fetch the price data. It is not linked to your email.
  • Images you upload to scan. The image is sent to our server and on to Anthropic, whose model is asked for one thing: the ticker and timeframe printed on the chart. We do not store the image. Anthropic handles it under its own terms (see the links below).
  • Usage analytics. We use PostHog, hosted in the EU, to see which pages and buttons get used. It does not record your screen, and it is not sent your tickers, your images or your email. It stores an anonymous identifier in your browser to tell visits apart.
  • Technical data. Our host, Cloudflare, processes your IP address and request details to deliver the site and block abuse. We keep a short-lived counter per IP address (about two minutes) to limit request rates.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your data.
  • We do not run advertising or ad tracking.
  • We do not record your sessions or your screen.
  • We never see your card number.

Who else handles data

These providers process data on our behalf, only to run ChartPro:

  • Cloudflare — hosting and delivery.
  • Resend — sends the sign-in code emails. Their privacy policy.
  • Dodo Payments — payments, tax and receipts, as the merchant of record. Their privacy policy.
  • Anthropic — reading the label off an uploaded chart image. Their privacy policy.
  • PostHog — product analytics. Their privacy policy.
  • Market-data providers (currently Yahoo Finance) — we ask them for price history for the ticker you typed. They receive the ticker, not who you are.

What is stored on your device

Your browser keeps your email (so Restore works), your onboarding answers and your saved watchlist. These stay on your device. Clearing your browser data removes them; your purchase itself is not lost, because it is tied to your email on our side.

How long we keep it

We keep your email, plan and credit balance for as long as your purchase is in effect and for any period we are required to keep purchase records. Price data is cached on our server for minutes to an hour and holds nothing about you.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, by emailing the address below. We will act on a verified request within 30 days. Deleting your email record means we can no longer restore a purchase for you, so do this only when you are finished with the service. If you are in the EU, UK or India you may also have rights under local data-protection law; those rights are covered by the same email.

Where data goes

Our providers operate internationally, so your data may be processed outside the country you are in. Analytics is hosted in the EU.

Not investment advice

ChartPro describes what a chart has already done — structure, levels, and the momentum behind a move. It does not tell you what to trade, or predict where price goes next. Technical analysis is educational; markets carry risk of loss.

Children

ChartPro is not directed at children. If you believe a child has given us an email address, tell us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
