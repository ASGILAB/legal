Privacy Policy — Fashion Astrology

Last updated: 8 August 2026

Fashion Astrology is made by ASGILAB.

The short version. Fashion Astrology does not collect, store or send your personal information anywhere. Your birth details, your wardrobe and the people you save all stay on your phone. There is no account, no login, and no server that holds your data.

What you give the app

To work out what to wear, the app needs to know where the planets were when you were born. So it asks for:

  • Your first name — used only to greet you on screen
  • Your birth date
  • Your birth city, chosen from a list built into the app
  • Your birth time, which is optional and works without

These are stored on your phone and are used to calculate your birth chart. They are never uploaded.

Your wardrobe

If you save clothes, you enter a name, a colour, a category and optionally a material. The app does not use your camera or your photo library and cannot see any of your photographs. Everything you save stays on the device.

People you save

If you use the feature that suggests what to wear when meeting someone, you can save that person's name and birth date. This is stored on your phone only. It is not uploaded, not shared, and not visible to the person concerned or to anyone else.

Where your birth city comes from

The list of cities is bundled inside the app, so choosing your birth place does not contact any server and does not reveal where you were born. The data comes from GeoNames and is used under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence.

Location

The app does not request or use your device's location. Your birth city is something you type in, not something the app detects.

Permissions we ask for

Notifications. Only if you switch on the morning reminder, and only to send that reminder. The reminder is created on your phone and does not pass through any server. If you say no, everything else works normally.

The app asks for no other permissions. No camera, no photos, no contacts, no location, no microphone.

Purchases

If you subscribe or buy the lifetime unlock, the purchase is handled by Apple and by RevenueCat, who manage subscriptions on our behalf.

RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier and details of the purchase itself so the app can tell that you have paid. It does not receive your name, your birth details, your wardrobe, or the people you have saved.

  • RevenueCat's privacy policy
  • Apple's privacy policy

We never see, and never store, your payment details.

Analytics and tracking

There are none. The app contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. We do not know how many times you open it, what it tells you, or what you decided to wear.

Deleting your data

Because nothing leaves your phone, deleting the app deletes everything. You can also clear your birth details at any time from Settings, and remove individual wardrobe items or saved people from their own screens.

Children

Fashion Astrology is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

A note on what this app is

Fashion Astrology is for entertainment. It makes no claim to predict events and offers no medical, psychological or financial advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
