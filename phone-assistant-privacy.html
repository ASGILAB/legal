Privacy Policy — Phone Assistant

Last updated: 3 August 2026

Phone Assistant is made by ASGILAB.

The short version. Phone Assistant does not collect, store, or send your personal information anywhere. Everything it checks about your phone stays on your phone. There is no account, no login, and no server that holds your data.

What the app reads from your phone

To tell you whether something needs attention, the app reads:

  • Battery level, and whether the phone is charging
  • Whether Low Power Mode is on
  • Whether the phone is running hot
  • How much storage space is free
  • Whether the phone is connected to Wi-Fi or mobile data
  • Screen brightness
  • How long since the phone was last restarted
  • Whether sound is being sent to Bluetooth earphones, a car, or a speaker
  • Whether Do Not Disturb or another Focus mode is on
  • Whether the VoiceOver screen reader is switched on

All of these are read on your phone and used only to draw the screen you are looking at. None of them are recorded, stored, or transmitted. Close the app and nothing is kept.

Permissions we ask for

Focus status. iOS requires your permission before any app can see whether Do Not Disturb is on. We ask for it so the app can tell you why your phone is not ringing. If you say no, that one check is skipped and everything else works normally.

Notifications. Used only for a single daily reminder to check your phone. Nothing else is ever sent.

Purchases

If you buy a subscription or the one-off unlock, the purchase is handled by Apple and by RevenueCat, who manage subscriptions on our behalf.

RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier and details of the purchase itself so it can tell the app that you have paid. It does not receive your name, your email address, or anything the app reads about your phone.

  • RevenueCat's privacy policy
  • Apple's privacy policy

We never see, and never store, your payment details.

Analytics and tracking

There are none. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. We do not know how many times you open it or what it tells you.

Children

Phone Assistant is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
