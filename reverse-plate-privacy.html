Privacy Policy — Reverse Plate

Last updated: 17 August 2026

Reverse Plate is made by ASGILAB.

The short version. Reverse Plate does not collect, store or send your personal information anywhere. There is no account, no login, and no server that holds your data. Everything you set stays on your phone.

The app never asks what you weigh

Reverse Plate has no field for your weight, your goal weight, your body mass index, your age or your sex. It does not ask, it cannot infer it, and there is nowhere for it to be stored.

This is a deliberate design decision rather than an omission. The app's job is to take the numbers you set and tell you what to cook. It has no opinion about your body and no reason to hold data about it, so it holds none.

The app does not log your food

There is no food search, no food diary, no barcode scanner and no camera access. Reverse Plate never asks for the camera, and the app is built so that it cannot request it.

You do not enter what you ate. You set targets, and the app suggests dishes that fit them.

What is stored, and where

All of the following is kept in the app's own storage on your device. None of it is transmitted, and none of it is readable by us or anyone else:

  • Your dial positions — calories, protein, carbohydrate, fat and fibre.
  • Your answers to the four questions asked when you first open the app: any diets you follow, cuisines you like, whether you are losing, holding or building, and how much cooking you want to do.
  • Foods you have asked the app never to suggest again.
  • The meals you added to today. These are held for the current day only and are cleared automatically. The app keeps no history of what you have eaten, and there is no way to look back at previous days.

Deleting the app deletes all of it. There is no backup on our side to restore from, because there was never a copy anywhere else.

The nutrition data

Food and nutrition figures come from the United States Department of Agriculture's FoodData Central, which is in the public domain. The data ships inside the app. Reverse Plate does not look food up over the internet, and it works with no network connection at all.

Subscriptions

Reverse Plate is a paid app with a free trial. Purchases are handled by Apple, and payment details are never seen by the app or by us.

To manage the subscription we use RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous identifier generated on your device together with the status of the purchase and basic device information such as the model and operating system version. This is used only to tell the app whether the subscription is active. It is not linked to your name, your email address or anything you have set in the app, and it is never used for advertising. RevenueCat's privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking

Reverse Plate contains no analytics SDK, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. It does not ask for permission to track you across other apps or websites, because it has no use for it. Nothing you do in the app is measured or reported.

Children

Reverse Plate is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Any change that meant the app started collecting something would be described here in plain words before it shipped.
