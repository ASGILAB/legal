Privacy Policy — Sastro

Last updated: 10 August 2026

Sastro is made by ASGILAB.

The short version. Sastro does not collect, store or send your personal information anywhere. Your birth details and your settings stay on your phone. There is no account, no login, and no server that holds your data.

What you give the app

To work out when you are at your best, the app needs to know where the planets were when you were born. So it asks for:

  • Your first name — used only to greet you on screen
  • Your birth date
  • Your birth city, chosen from a list built into the app
  • Your birth time, which is optional and works without
  • What you make, and the hours you are usually free to post

These are stored on your phone and are used to calculate your birth chart. They are never uploaded.

We are not connected to your social accounts

Sastro does not connect to Instagram, TikTok, YouTube or any other platform. It does not ask you to sign in to them, it cannot see your posts, your followers, your analytics or your messages, and it has no access to any account you hold anywhere.

Everything the app tells you is calculated from your birth chart and the positions of the planets. Nothing about your actual accounts is read, because nothing about them is available to it.

Where your birth city comes from

The list of cities is bundled inside the app, so choosing your birth place does not contact any server and does not reveal where you were born. The data comes from GeoNames and is used under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence.

Location

The app does not request or use your device's location. Your birth city is something you type in, not something the app detects.

Permissions we ask for

Notifications. Only if you switch on the morning notification, and only to send that notification. It is created on your phone and does not pass through any server. If you say no, everything else works normally.

The app asks for no other permissions. No camera, no photos, no contacts, no location, no microphone.

Purchases

If you subscribe or buy the lifetime unlock, the purchase is handled by Apple and by RevenueCat, who manage subscriptions on our behalf.

RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier and details of the purchase itself so the app can tell that you have paid. It does not receive your name, your birth details, or anything else you have entered.

  • RevenueCat's privacy policy
  • Apple's privacy policy

We never see, and never store, your payment details.

Analytics and tracking

There are none. The app contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. We do not know how many times you open it, what it tells you, or whether you posted.

Deleting your data

Because nothing leaves your phone, deleting the app deletes everything. You can also clear your birth details at any time from Settings using "Start over".

Children

Sastro is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

A note on what this app is

Sastro is for entertainment. It describes your own rhythm — when you are likely to be at your best — and it makes no claim to predict what any social platform will do with anything you post. It offers no medical, psychological or financial advice, and it does not promise any audience, reach or income.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
