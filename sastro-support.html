What Sastro does

Sastro reads your birth chart against today's sky and turns it into a working plan: the hours you are most likely to be at your best, what suits the day, and the one thing to leave alone.

What it does not do

It does not predict what any social platform will do with your posts. Nobody can see inside those systems, and an app claiming otherwise is guessing. Sastro describes your rhythm — when you are most willing to be on camera, most likely to write well, most inclined to go back through your archive — which is something you can check against your own experience.

It also has no connection to your accounts. It cannot see your posts, your followers or your analytics, and it never asks you to log in anywhere.

Common questions

I don't know my birth time. Can I still use it?

Yes, and plenty of people are in the same position. Choose "I don't know my birth time" during setup. You still get the planetary hours, the Moon's sign, the phase, retrogrades and four of the eight Blueprint readings.

What you lose is the houses, which is where a lot of the day-to-day detail comes from. Those readings are left out rather than guessed at. If you find your birth time later — it is usually on your birth certificate — add it from Settings and the app gets noticeably sharper.

My birth town isn't in the list.

The bundled city list covers places above roughly 15,000 people, so smaller towns are missing. Pick the nearest city that is listed. The time zone and coordinates will be near-identical and your chart will not meaningfully change.

The recommended time is no good for me.

Tap any hour on the Today timeline and you get a straight verdict for that hour — Peak, Good, Fine or Skip. The app is designed never to leave you with nothing useful to do.

Why does it tell me not to post at certain times?

Some hours genuinely suit other work better. A Saturn hour is for editing, culling and planning rather than publishing. An app where every hour is fine would not be worth opening.

What happens during Mercury retrograde?

Sastro does not treat it as dead time. The traditional reading of a retrograde is the "re-" prefix — revisit, revise, revive — so the app points you at your archive: repurpose, re-cut something that worked, fix your bio. It will advise against launching or signing anything during that window.

The advice doesn't match what I make.

Check Settings → What you make. Every instruction is phrased for your medium, so a photographer and a podcaster get different wording from the same planetary hour. Change it there and the whole app re-reads immediately.

Can I turn the astrology off?

Yes. Settings → Show the astrology hides the small reason lines. The advice itself does not change — the app still works out every recommendation the same way, it just stops showing the reasoning.

Light or dark?

Settings → Theme. Auto follows your phone. Sastro is designed for dark, but both work.

Subscription

How does the free trial work?

Three days free on either subscription, then it renews at the price shown. Cancel at any point in the first three days and you are not charged. The lifetime option is a one-time purchase and never renews.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Sastro → Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends.

I paid but the app is still locked.

Tap Restore purchases on the paywall or in Settings. Make sure the phone is signed in to the same Apple ID you bought it with. If that does not fix it, email us with the date of purchase and we will sort it out.

Refunds

Apple handles all refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue refunds ourselves, but tell us what went wrong and we will try to fix it.

Notifications aren't arriving

Check that they are enabled in iOS Settings → Sastro → Notifications, then confirm the morning notification is switched on in the app's own Settings. If permission was denied the first time, it has to be granted from iOS Settings — the app cannot ask again.

Deleting your data

Everything is stored on your phone, so deleting the app removes all of it. To start fresh without deleting, use Settings → Start over.
